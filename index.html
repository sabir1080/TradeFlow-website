Questions

Things people ask before buying.

Straight answers. If your question is not here, just message us.

Is my business data safe?
Yes. TradeFlow runs on your own machine or your own server, so your data stays with you — it is not held by a third party. Access is controlled by user roles, every significant change is written to an audit log, and the built-in backup and restore tools let you keep regular copies of the database.
Do I need to know accounting to use it?
No. That is the point of the design. You record normal business actions — a sale, a purchase, a payment — and TradeFlow creates the correct double-entry postings behind the scenes. Your accountant gets a proper Trial Balance, P&L and Balance Sheet without you having to think in debits and credits.
Can TradeFlow be customized for my business?
Yes. TradeFlow already has business-wide configuration for company details, tax codes, fiscal periods and account mapping. Beyond that, custom modules, custom reports and workflow changes built specifically for how you operate are available as add-ons on top of any package — priced after a short requirement review.
Can I move my existing data into TradeFlow?
In most cases yes. Item lists, customer and supplier records and opening balances can be imported from Excel or from your current system. Data migration is included with the TradeFlow ERP package and can be quoted separately for the others, once we see the format of your existing data.
Is training and support included?
Every package includes setup, basic configuration and initial training. On a Perpetual licence, that also includes 3 months of free maintenance and technical support after go-live — covering bug fixes, technical support and troubleshooting of existing functionality. After the 3 months, continued support is optional: you can renew it yearly through an Annual Maintenance & Support (AMC) contract, but you are never required to. New features, custom development and major enhancements are always quoted separately, whether or not you hold an AMC. Subscription-licence customers keep maintenance, support and updates running for as long as their subscription stays active.
Does it work with multiple users at the same time?
Yes. TradeFlow supports multiple users with role-based permissions, so a counter operator, a store keeper and an accountant each see the parts of the system relevant to their job. Every package includes granular, permission-based access control.
Does it run online or offline?
TradeFlow can be deployed locally on your own network so it keeps working even when the internet is down — important for a retail counter — or hosted so it is reachable from multiple sites. We will recommend the right setup based on how many locations you run.
How long does implementation take?
TradeFlow Business can usually go live within a few days. TradeFlow ERP typically takes one to three weeks depending on how much historical data has to be migrated and how much training is needed. Add-on custom modules are scheduled after the requirement review.
Is the price one-time or a monthly subscription?
Either — you choose. A Perpetual licence is a one-time purchase: software delivery, setup and 3 months of free maintenance & support after deployment, then an optional yearly AMC if you want support to continue — it is never a lifetime free-support commitment. A Subscription licence is a lower setup fee plus a monthly payment, with maintenance, support and updates continuing for as long as the subscription is active, and no source-code delivery. Final pricing is confirmed after we understand your requirements.
What if I need a completely different system?
Then we build it. Alongside TradeFlow we develop custom software — HR and payroll, attendance, school management, clinic systems, warehouse applications and full custom ERPs. If you can describe the workflow clearly, it can be built.
